# Degree project
**Validated through user research, with 75% of participants (6 out of 8) confirming the solution would help them maintain focus.**


### Overview
#### Think Beyond AI
Jojo is a personalized AI companion designed to encourage active thinking, improve focus, and help users regain balance in their digital lives. Unlike traditional AI tools that passively provide solutions, Jojo creates an engaging and interactive experience by integrating human-like interaction features and fostering critical thinking.

### The Goal
#### Use digital technology without a harmful cycle of dependency
Since coexistence with digital technology is inevitable, my goal is to identify ways to use it effectively and responsibly, avoiding a harmful cycle of dependency.

### Design
#### Target User
The primary target users are young adults who want to use AI tools in a smart and sustainable way, but are concerned about becoming too dependent on them.

### User Interview
User interviews on AI tool use, pain points, and needs.

### Unpacking the Solution 01
#### Problem 1 — Cognitive focus
Difficulty maintaining cognitive focus and sustained engagement in digital environments.

Cognitive challenges caused by digital technology are real, and many people rely on AI tools to help them stay focused and manage their tasks.

* **Activeness Mode:** Stimulate deeper thought and active engagement without user prompts. JoJo runs in the background, using screen activity tracking, face recognition, and eye tracking to analyze user focus, confusion, or hesitation.
* **Question prompts instead of direct answers:** JoJo proactively initiates thought-provoking questions tailored to the user's context, encouraging them to think critically rather than simply absorb information.

### Unpacking the Solution 02
#### Problem 2 — Abstract progress
Cognitive thinking and engagement are abstract, which makes it hard for users to gauge their progress or improvement.

* **Emotional AI character:** An AI character that can express emotions through facial expressions based on the conversation flow. The character's expressions help users stay focused and emotionally connected to the topic.
* **Intuitive reporting:** An intuitive reporting system that measures both cognitive thinking and engagement levels. The report translates complex data into clear, visual scores, then offers personalized feedback and motivational encouragement.

### Unpacking the Solution 03
#### Report and goals
JoJo's report is designed to help you build better habits with technology by tracking how you think, focus, and interact throughout your day.

Each circle represents a day's data. When you hover over a circle, you can see detailed data for that day on the right side. The red-filled circle highlights today's data.

### Takeaway
#### Stay the master of the creative process
AI has already become deeply embedded in our daily lives, and we will likely coexist with it indefinitely. Rather than simply using it, we must study and cultivate the right habits to integrate it meaningfully into our existence.

Technological advancement doesn't necessarily make us lazy, but it can lead to cognitive offloading. This isn't a form of human devolution, but rather a side effect of tool dependency. If we consciously define AI as an intellectual lever rather than a crutch, it becomes a golden opportunity for creative expansion.

Through this research, I have gained deep insights into how technology affects our cognitive abilities. To thrive in the long term, we must maintain a conscious awareness of how we use these tools, ensuring we remain the masters of the creative process.
